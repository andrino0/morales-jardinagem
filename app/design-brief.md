# Morales Jardinagem — website design brief

## Design read
A practical local gardening service for homeowners and property managers in Pelotas and nearby areas. The experience should feel reliable, capable, energetic and close to the community, never like a generic landscaping franchise.

## Concept spine
**A garden brought back to life.** The entire page follows the visible change from overgrown edges to a clean, cared-for outdoor space. The electric trimmer, the cut line, leaves and fresh lawn become the recurring visual language.

## Delivery tier
Cinema, with one continuous scroll-scrub film as the signature interaction. All motion has a reduced-motion fallback and mobile is framed first.

## Locked visual system
- Theme paradigm: Bold Studio Solid, forest green.
- Palette: deep bottle green #153F30, secondary garden green #285B43, leaf green #72B84A, warm white #F5F8F1, brand yellow #E2EC55. Yellow is the single accent; the greens are the brand's structural colors.
- Defense: these colors preserve the Morales logo and existing uniform identity while avoiding a generic dark-tech treatment.
- Typography: Outfit for display and body, IBM Plex Mono for small factual labels.
- Background character: cinematic garden imagery with deep green scrims, tactile leaf/grass details and generous negative space.
- Hero architecture: massive image-first with restrained text, centered safe focal point.
- Section system: cinematic journey, asymmetric service composition, editorial image-and-copy, wide local service statement, oversized contact finale.
- Signature components: long index rows with sliding numerals, cinematic photo edge blending, full-width locality image canvas, pressed-stamp contact action.
- Narrative spine: living system / garden.
- Second-read moment: one macro crop of a clean cut edge carrying the brand yellow.

## Animation mode
Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey block
- Journey shape: single-shot. One unbroken 15-second camera move through one garden, not a chain of separate worlds.
- Journey chapters: (1) a close view of the electric trimmer at the lawn edge, (2) the clean cut line opening through the grass, (3) the camera gliding past trimmed borders and a tidy yard, (4) the garden opens into its finished, cared-for state.
- World grammar: photorealistic Brazilian residential garden, deep bottle-green shadows, natural late-afternoon sunlight, fresh grass and foliage, restrained brand-yellow glints, one continuous slow forward camera move, locked exposure and white balance, no cuts, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
- Mobile framing: keep the cutting head, grass edge and final garden within the central 60% of frame; use a center-safe crop and lighter 720px mobile encode.
- Film delivery budget: desktop ≤32 MiB; mobile ≤16 MiB.
- The journey enacts the concept by physically moving from detail-level care to the overall finished space.

## Section plan
1. Home journey — cinematic image-first hero with a short service promise and direct WhatsApp action.
2. Services — diagonal staggered masonry: roçada de terrenos, limpeza de quintais, jardinagem, pequenos reparos, manutenção de jardins.
3. Care in the details — layered image crops and concise copy about careful finishing and electric equipment.
4. Local service — full-width image-as-canvas statement: Pelotas e região. Do not invent a list of cities or service radius.
5. Contact — oversized dark-green contact finale with the single primary intent “Solicitar orçamento”.

## CTA inventory
- Navigation: compact text link, “Orçamento”.
- Hero: brand-yellow contact plate with a WhatsApp icon and “Pedir orçamento”.
- Services: each item is informational, not a CTA.
- Local section: inline locality link is not a separate sales intent.
- Contact finale: bespoke stamp-style WhatsApp action, “Solicitar orçamento”.
- Footer: plain WhatsApp number link, same intent and same label.

## Copy constraints
Use Brazilian Portuguese. Never claim gasoline equipment; Morales works with electric brushcutters and a 40-metre extension for larger areas. Do not invent customer reviews, years in business, prices, performance statistics, completed-project photographs or a list of nearby towns. Generated garden imagery is illustrative, not presented as a real client project.

## Anti-convergence ledger
No earlier Morales website exists in this build. The identity axes are set here: forest-green brand palette, Outfit + IBM Plex Mono, massive image-first hero, A4 scroll-scrub, bespoke contact plate + stamped finale, and clipped/diagonal garden crop language.
